# Moodboard - Arise Soul and the Arise Galaxy (Stage 3)

Status: provisional. The reference images are held outside the repository (`../arise soul/`) and are **not copied** here; this page describes them and what the direction takes from each. Nothing in the references is reproduced verbatim; all shipped art is generated by `tools/assets/*.js`.

## Reference 1 - chosen Arise Soul in a lab tank (`arise soul art.png`)

What it shows: a component-generator UI (FACE / EYES / BUBBLE / BODY / BELLY / ANTENNA tabs) and, on the right, a soft translucent blue-white droplet creature inside a glowing cyan tank. Round white head with heart-shaped eyes, a small orb on a stalk with a thin ring around it, a large pink heart in the belly, a body that flares like a droplet and ends in two rounded stubby legs; white bubble highlights float in the translucent blue.

Taken: **the anatomy and modularity.** Round head, antenna orb, belly mark, stubby feet, translucent body with white highlight blobs, and the idea that a Soul is assembled from independent parts. Our six modular slots (body, palette, eyes, antenna, mouth, glow) mirror this structure.

Not taken: the "NFT generator" framing, rarity/trial language, the lab tank as a permanent frame (the tank becomes a brief entrance glow only), and heart-eyes as a default (eyes are a neutral set: dot, star, sleepy, wide, wink, sparkle).

## Reference 2 - clean Soul sprite (`Arise soul 1 (2).png`)

What it shows: the same droplet creature as a clean sprite on white; light blue translucent body, a whiter inner head sphere, stalk + orb antenna, white bubble patches, two rounded feet.

Taken: **the silhouette proportions** (head about a third of the height, body widest just above the feet), the inner white "face disc" inside a translucent outer body, the highlight-blob treatment. The `lagoon` palette is the closest match to this sprite and is the palette used in the trait rows of the contact sheet.

## References 3 and 4 - holographic pastel-neon creatures (`38d8...jpg`, `4285...jpg`)

What they show: cat-eared blob creatures on pure black, filled with iridescent pastel gradients (cyan, mint, lilac, pink), drawn with very thick violet outlines, star-shaped eyes, dripping edges, and small four-point sparkles around them.

Taken: **the finish on the dark Galaxy.** Thick soft outline in a deep hue of the same palette, a blurred glow stroke behind the outline, star and sparkle eye variants, four-point twinkles as constellation glyphs, and pastel (not saturated neon) fills so eight palettes can sit in one luminance band.

Not taken: cat ears and animal features (they read as a species and invite "which animal are you" stereotyping), drips (they read as decay/melting), cursor/arrow props, and pure black backgrounds (we use deep navy `#0B0F1F` so the outline hue still separates from the background).

## Derived direction in one line

*A friendly translucent droplet companion - round head, antenna, heart belly, stubby feet - drawn with a thick soft outline and a pastel glow, floating in a slow, deep-navy galaxy where every Soul is the same size and the same brightness.*
