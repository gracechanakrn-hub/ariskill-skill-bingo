# Arise Galaxy Scene Specification (Stage 3.1)

Status: submitted, provisional (OD-12 style, OD-14 venue). Owner: art-director-agent. Data: `assets/scene/*` generated by `tools/assets/build-scene.js` from `assets-src/scene/scene-params.json`. Consumer: public-galaxy workstream (13.4). Renderer order (D-13): WebGL2 -> Canvas 2D -> static.

## 1. Scene hierarchy (back to front)

```
GalaxyViewport (clip: x in [0, 1344) at 1920x1080 since the 70 / 30 split, D-52 - [0, 1248) before; scales with viewport height)
  0 Background        flat --color-bg #0B0F1F
  1 Nebula            assets/scene/nebula.svg  (4 soft radial blobs, violet/blue/teal, <= 38 % opacity)
  2 Star layer 1 far  420 stars, parallax 0.15, twinkle +-8 % / 9 s
  3 Star layer 2 mid  220 stars, parallax 0.35, twinkle +-12 % / 7 s
  4 Star layer 3 near  90 stars, parallax 0.60, twinkle +-15 % / 5 s
  5 Orbit rings       9 dashed ellipses (aspect 1.35), 14 % opacity, from orbit-rings.json
  6 Souls             sprites at full / simple / dot LOD, sorted by ring (outer first) then slot
  7 Entrance layer    active Float arcs, pulse, short trail (high level only)
  8 Foreground glow   one very soft vignette-glow towards the viewport centre, <= 12 % opacity
InfoPanel (x >= 1344): opaque, no scene content
```

Nothing from layers 1-8 is drawn at x >= panelX (1344 at 1920 wide, `scene-config.json#safeZone`). All layer positions are normalised (0..1 of the Galaxy viewport) so the scene scales to any 16:9 display.

## 2. Camera

- Fixed orthographic camera, no zoom, no roll.
- Slow bounded drift: centre moves on a Lissajous path with radius 1.5 % of viewport height, period 40 s (`camera.driftRadiusFraction`, `driftPeriodSeconds`). Parallax layers move by `parallax * drift`.
- Max angular speed 0.5 deg/s for any camera-driven motion; **shake is forbidden** (`camera.shake = "forbidden"`), including on winner.
- Reduced motion: camera static.

## 3. Lighting and materials (2D sprite pipeline)

- No dynamic lights. "Lighting" is baked into the Soul SVG: vertical highlight-to-fill gradient, white specular blobs, blurred glow stroke.
- Blend modes: stars and glows use additive ("lighter") blending in WebGL2/Canvas 2D; Souls use normal alpha so outlines stay crisp.
- Textures: each Soul is rasterised once per (config, detail, size bucket) from `renderSoulSvg` into an atlas (size buckets 32 / 64 / 128 / 192 px); atlas <= 4096^2 RGBA (64 MB budget). Nebula is rasterised once at viewport size.
- Colour: all Soul colours come from `PALETTES`; the scene never tints a Soul (tinting could encode status).

## 4. Spawn / entrance behaviour (P-05)

Trigger: one `soul.float_accepted` event per `soulId` (dedup; replays and reconnect snapshots never animate).

1. **Enter** from the stage-configured edge (`entrance.edges`, default `bottom`; per-event value from `SoulEntrance.edge`), starting 8 % outside the viewport, at `full` LOD regardless of count.
2. **Arc** for 2200 ms along a quadratic curve towards the assigned orbit slot, ease `--ease-standard`, with a short additive trail (high level only).
3. **Pulse** once for 600 ms at the slot: glow opacity 0.55 -> 1.0 -> 0.55, scale 1.0 -> 1.12 -> 1.0 (the only scale change ever applied to a Soul, and it is transient and identical for everyone).
4. **Settle** for 1200 ms into the ring's LOD and angular speed.

At most 6 concurrent entrances; further entrances queue; if the queue exceeds 12, extra Souls settle instantly (`entrance.queueOverflow`). Reduced motion: 400 ms fade at the slot.

## 5. Orbit slot allocation

`soulOrbitSlot(soulId, count)` in `packages/ui/soul-renderer.js` (mirrored in `assets/scene/orbit-rings.json`):

- 9 rings, radii 0.12 … 0.92 of the viewport half-height, ellipse aspect 1.35 (x wider), capacities 6 / 14 / 24 / 36 / 52 / 72 / 96 / 128 / 160 = **588 slots**.
- Rings open from the inside as `count` grows (cumulative capacity >= count); the ring is chosen by hash weighted by capacity, the slot by a second hash, the angle = slot centre + +-0.25 slot jitter. Deterministic per soulId; a slot collision is resolved by the renderer scanning to the next free slot in the same ring (state kept client-side, deterministic given the same ordered snapshot).
- Each ring rotates slowly: ring 0 at 0.02 rad/s, decreasing 6 % per ring outward; each Soul also bobs +-1.2 % of viewport height with phase `driftPhase`.
- **Clusters above 300 Souls**: when count > `orbit.clusterAbove` (300), rings 7-8 are drawn as aggregate clusters: Souls are bucketed into 96 px grid cells and rendered as one `dot` sprite per cell with a small count label ("x 12") in `--color-text-muted`; the newest Float still enters individually and then joins its cluster. No cluster is larger or brighter than another beyond its count label.
- LOD: `full` when count <= 120 or sprite >= 56 px; `simple` when count <= 300 or sprite >= 24 px; `dot` otherwise (`scene-config.json#lod`). Entrances always use `full`.

## 6. Performance levels

| Level | Max Souls | Star layers | Nebula | Rings | Glow filters | Twinkle | Trail | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| high | 600 | 3 | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | 60 fps |
| medium | 450 | 2 | yes | yes | no (pre-baked) | yes | no | 60 fps |
| low | 300 | 1 | yes | no | no | no | no | 30 fps |

Auto-downgrade when the 5 s average drops below 45 fps, with a 30 s cooldown (`performance.autoDowngrade`); never auto-upgrade during Playing. Level is also settable from the public URL (`?quality=low`) for rehearsal.

Frame-time budget: 16.7 ms at 60 fps; **30-minute soak at 450 Souls on `medium` must hold >= 30 fps** (the acceptance capture on venue hardware is owed by workstream 13.4 / QA; not producible in this stage). Budgets: texture memory <= 64 MB, JS bundle <= 350 KB gzip, scene data <= 120 KB (current 49.6 KB; the D-36 space pack is texture, not scene data: section 13), Soul SVG <= 6 KB.

## 7. Reduced-motion mode

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` or `?motion=reduced`: orbit static, parallax off, twinkle off, camera static, glow pulse off, entrances = 400 ms fade at the final slot, winner = single 800 ms bloom on the panel only. Composition is otherwise identical so the state is still legible.

## 8. 2D fallback and static mode (P-07)

- **Canvas 2D**: same layers, no filters (Souls at `simple` unless count <= 60), stars as pre-rendered sprites, additive blending via `globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter'`.
- **Static**: `nebula.svg` + star layer 1 as a static image, Souls placed by `soulOrbitSlot` at `simple` detail, no animation; still updates on each snapshot (Souls appear with no transition).
- Fallback never touches game state; the panel is independent of the renderer and keeps working if the Galaxy throws (error boundary shows the flat background).

## 9. Information safe zone

Right 30 % (`x >= 1344` at 1920; D-52, 35 % before). Effects clipped; panel opaque; 32 px inner margin; typography from `.theme-venue`. Tested in `tools/assets/test/souls.test.js` (scene-config values) and must be visually verified in every public state at rehearsal.

## 11. Deterministic asset generation and export rules

- All scene files come from `node tools/assets/build-scene.js`; inputs are `assets-src/scene/scene-params.json` and `ORBIT_RINGS` from the renderer (single source). Star positions use `hash32(seed:index:salt)` so the same seed reproduces the same sky; running twice produces identical bytes (verified by diff).
- Files: `starfield-layer-{1,2,3}.json` (normalised x, y, radius px @1080, brightness, twinkle phase), `nebula.svg` (galaxy width x 1080, clip-path to the Galaxy viewport), `orbit-rings.json`, `scene-config.json`. Manifest entries with sha256 checksums are merged into `assets/manifest/assets.yaml`.
- Runtime format: JSON + SVG (no PNG; renderers rasterise at load). Formats are recorded in the manifest (`runtime_format`).
- Never hand-edit `assets/scene/**`; change `scene-params.json` and regenerate. Tests compare the manifest checksums to the files.

## 12. Handoff to the public-galaxy workstream

- Load `assets/scene/scene-config.json` first; it links the other files and carries `safeZone`, `camera`, `entrance`, `lod`, `performance`, `reducedMotion`, `fallback`, `budgets`.
- Render Souls with `renderSoulSvg(entrance.config, { detail, badge: entrance.badge, reducedMotion, idPrefix: entrance.soulId })`; place with `soulOrbitSlot(entrance.soulId, galaxy.count)`.
- Do not add any per-Soul scale, tint, glow or ordering derived from data other than `config`, `badge` and `soulId`.

## 13. Stage 15 (D-36): the living Galaxy and the space backdrop

The big screen replaced layers 0-1 above, and the doodads, forest, fireflies and aurora, with a cinematic space sky, and
gave the Souls depth and a little life. `nebula.svg`, `doodads.svg` and their modules are still generated and kept for
