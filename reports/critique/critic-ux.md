## 3. Strengths to preserve

- **25-selection flow is fast and honest.** Persistent `N / 25` progress bar, tap-to-fill, chips with Remove and one-level Undo, per-row `Can be drawn` / `Not in the draw pool` and Core/Critical/Optional icon + label, autosave with E6 retry, automatic server finalize at 25, and an explicit reshuffle warning before editing. No accept-all control anywhere (verified in `views/select.js`).
- **Lock vs start is unambiguous without narration.** "Locks at HH:MM" plus a tabular `mm:ss` countdown, E7 "Syncing with the event..." when unsynced, then a lock seal and "Board locked. Waiting for the host to start..." before any draw.
- **Draws 1-4 rule reads instantly** on all three surfaces: "Bingo unlocks at draw 5" -> green "Bingo is live" pill at draw 5, with the draw card (number, name, kind) and `N matched on your board`.
- **Result and Soul avoid capability language**: "Bingo detected / Round complete", co-winner count, "This game is about discovery, not assessment.", private insight card marked "Only you can see this.", Bingo Star with a text equivalent, READY TO FLOAT with a server-clocked 3-2-1 and E13 retry on the same token.
- **Recovery coverage**: E1-E23 all have a reachable, correctly-worded surface except E19 (UX-04); reconnect banners keep the last frame on mobile, admin, public and stage.
- **Admin is safe under pressure where it matters most**: Next Skill has a busy lock, pending label and post-draw announcement; stale E10 refreshes Mission Control; Start now confirms; Cancel demands a reason; readiness panel shows E20 and the 60 s overdue warning; audit tab is one click away.
- **Public and stage legibility at distance**: 96 px+ skill headline with fit-to-box shrinking, icon + label + colour for kind, QR at 420 px with a human-readable URL, previous winners, and stage cohort suppression labelled "Small groups hidden for privacy".
