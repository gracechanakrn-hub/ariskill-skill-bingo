## 3. Strengths to preserve

- The admin flow is safe by construction: one command in flight, idempotency keys, `expectedVersion`, E10 refresh, confirmation for Start now, mandatory cancel reason, and no auto-start anywhere. Double-click and two-tab cases are covered by tests and by the limiter.
- Server-side timers (`timers.js`) make lock, overdue and result-window transitions independent of any client being open - the right design for a venue with flaky laptops.
- The runbook's incident table (section 6) is short, symptom-first and honest ("never edit winners"); section 8 already anticipates the NAT rate-limit trap.
- Rehearsal script is checklist-shaped with observer/time/decision fields and includes the seeded deterministic round (18.2) - keep this as the go/no-go artefact.
- Rollback principle "never roll back across a schema migration; cancel transparently, never recompute winners" is exactly right.
